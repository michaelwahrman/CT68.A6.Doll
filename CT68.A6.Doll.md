# CT68.A6.Doll

A6 of the three MIDI-controlled caroler dolls. It is a copy of CT68.A5.Doll, which is unchanged, and everything from A5 still works. For the rest, see `..\CT68.A5.Doll\CT68.A5.Doll.md` and the earlier notes.

## What A6 changes

1. **"The hair on the top of the head looks wrong when it is partly transparent."**
   - The curls keep their 0.60 opacity. The cap of hair over the top and crown of the head is now always solid.
   - They were already separate meshes (`hair_cap` and `curl_*`) with separate materials, so only the cap's material changed. In `src/doll_rig.js` the opacity setting now goes to the curls only.
   - The same split is in the Arnold export: the `hair` shader has no opacity and is opaque, and the `curl` shader has `opacity 0.6`.
   - In the panel, the slider is now called **Curl opacity**. The default style is **Back, 0.55 thick, curls 0.60 opacity, solid top (A6 default)**. The other styles (A4 solid, full, semi-transparent, A3) are unchanged, but their opacity is now the curls' only.
2. **"Start with the panel visible and singing enabled."**
   - The page opens with the controls panel shown (A5 opened with it hidden). **H** or **Hide ✕** still hides it.
   - The dolls are singing when the page opens. **S**, the **Stop singing** button or CC 29 stops them.
3. **"Show the version in the app."**
   - The top left of the window reads **CT68.A6.Doll**, in brighter type, before the key help. It shows with the panel hidden too.
   - The page title (the browser tab) is `CT68.A6.Doll`.
   - The version is set in one place: `VERSION` near the end of `src/doll_rig.js`. `build.mjs` puts it in the page title, the page sets the label from it, and `doll_export.mjs` puts it in the `.ass` and `.obj` headers. For A7, change that one line, then run `node build.mjs` and `node doll_export.mjs`.

The browser keeps its saved settings under new A6 keys. Your MIDI map and noise channels are carried over from A5. The hair starts at the A6 default.

## Run it

- **Browser:** double-click `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\CT68.A6.Doll\dolls_midi.html` in Chrome or Edge.
  - Or run the `ct68-a6` entry in `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\.claude\launch.json` (port 8787), and open http://127.0.0.1:8787/dolls_midi.html.
- **Keys:** S sing, T talk, B blink, P spin, N noise, R record, H hide panel, V start view.
- **Arnold:** the exports are in `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\CT68.A6.Doll_exports`, as with A5. Copy the ones you want into `CT68.A6.Doll\exports\`, then run `kick -i exports/dolls_rest.ass` from the `CT68.A6.Doll` folder.
  - To rebuild them: `node doll_export.mjs --out ..\CT68.A6.Doll_exports` (or with no `--out`, into `exports\`).

## Tested

**In the built-in browser:**

- **The page:** loads with no console errors. The tab title and the label both read CT68.A6.Doll.
- **At start:** the panel is shown, the button reads **Stop singing** and the status reads "Singing.".
- **The hair:** on all three dolls `hair_cap` is opacity 1 and not dithered, and the curls are opacity 0.6 and dithered. In a close view of the head, the top of the hair is solid.

**Exports:** `node doll_export.mjs --out ..\CT68.A6.Doll_exports` wrote every pose in `poses\` (`blink`, `glance`, `raised`, `rest`, `rest_sheer`, `sing`: `.ass`, `_uv.ass`, `.obj`). In `dolls_rest.ass` the three `curl` shaders have `opacity 0.6`, and the `hair` shaders have no opacity and are opaque: 6 `opaque off` shapes, against 9 in A5.
